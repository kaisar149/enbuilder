# World builder: quick guide (Part 9b)

1. Start the server: `wb-server --preset earthlike` (add `--solve` to compute everything at start). Open the address it prints.
2. **Show on map** chooses what the map shows. Greyed layers need a solve first.
3. **Solve** computes the world. The bar shows progress; Cancel stops it.
4. **World** switches presets and shows the shape and cell count.
5. Map: drag to pan, scroll to zoom at the cursor, double click (or 0) to reset. Hover reads a value; click fills **Selected cell** with every value for that cell.
6. The legend under the map follows the chosen layer, in display units (degrees C, mm/day).
