# User guide: the 3D world (Part 9d)

Click **3D world** at the top left of the map (and **2D map** to go back). The layer, season, selected cell and legend stay the same.

- **Turn the world:** drag. **Zoom:** mouse wheel (or + and -). **Pan:** right-drag or shift-drag. **Centre a cell:** double-click it. **Reset view:** the button, or the Home key. Arrow keys also turn the world.
- **View panel (left):** *Relief exaggeration* raises land by elevation (x1 is true scale, 0 is flat; the sea stays flat). *Lighting*: Flat shows exactly the map colours; Sun lights the world from its star, and follows the Season slider. *Day and night* darkens the night side; *Time of day* turns the planet under the sun.
- **Hover** a cell for its biome, the shown layer and the main values such as temperature and rainfall. **Click** to select; the outline and the Selected cell panel follow.
- **Measure frame rate** spins the view for 3 seconds and reports frames per second on your computer.

Shapes: a globe for sphere worlds, a cube for cube worlds, a tilted plane for flat worlds. If your browser has no WebGL the view stays empty and the panel says so.

Limits: relief only shows the elevation field; colours are always one flat colour per cell.
